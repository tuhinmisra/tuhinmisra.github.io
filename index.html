Two ways of designing

A new aircraft gives freedom to choose every parameter. A modified aircraft demands discipline, because the existing structure, systems, and certification history shape what is possible. Both teach something the other cannot.

✎

Clean-sheet design

Starting from requirements, I size the wing, tail, and propulsion system, then iterate until range, endurance, speed, and payload agree with one another.

Modification and adaptation

Existing airframes can take on new roles through new payload bays, wing changes, or different propulsion. The challenge is to gain capability without losing stability or margin.
